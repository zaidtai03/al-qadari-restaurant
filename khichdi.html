What is Desi Ghee Khichdi?

Desi Ghee Khichdi is comfort food in its purest, most authentic form. It's a slow-cooked blend of lentils (dal) and rice cooked together, then finished generously with pure desi ghee (Indian clarified butter). This simple yet profound dish represents the heart of Gujarati home cooking — wholesome, nourishing, and deeply satisfying.

Why It's Special

🫒 Pure Desi Ghee
The soul of this dish is the generous amount of pure desi ghee that crowns it. This isn't just an ingredient — it's a celebration of authentic Gujarati flavors.

💛 Comfort & Nourishment
Khichdi is known in Indian culture as the ultimate comfort food. It's what mothers make when family is sick, what grandmothers have perfected over decades, and what brings people together around the dinner table. Al Qadri's version honors this legacy while elevating it to restaurant quality.

⏱️ Slow-Cooked to Perfection
Our khichdi is not rushed. The lentils and rice are cooked together slowly, allowing them to merge and create a creamy, comforting texture. This takes time and attention — exactly what our chefs give it.

🌾 Authentic Gujarati Recipe
This is a true Gujarati classic done with love. The spice level is balanced — warming without being overwhelming. The texture is creamy without being mushy. Every element is in perfect harmony.
