What is Mamna?

Mamna is Al Qadri's most cherished signature dish and the reason Bilimora knows our name. It's not just chicken — it's a legacy on a plate. This iconic dish features tender, succulent pieces of chicken that have been slow-marinated in our exclusive blend of spices and then cooked to perfection.

Why People Love Mamna

🏆 House Favourite
Mamna is the dish that built Al Qadri's reputation. For over a decade, this is what our loyal customers keep coming back for.

✨ Secret Family Recipe
Mamna comes from a cherished family recipe that's been perfected over years. The exact blend of spices is known only to Irsad Tai and Sama Tai. This exclusivity makes Mamna truly unique and impossible to replicate elsewhere.

🌶️ Perfect Flavor Balance
The magic lies in the balance — rich spices that warm you up, tender chicken that melts in your mouth, and a sauce that's not too heavy. It's comfort food elevated to restaurant quality.

💪 Authentic Quality
We use only the finest, farm-sourced chicken hand-selected for quality. Every bird is marinated for hours to ensure the flavors penetrate deeply. No shortcuts, no compromises — just pure authentic taste.
