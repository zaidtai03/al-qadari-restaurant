What is Kaleji?

Kaleji (liver) is a traditional dish in Indian cuisine, particularly popular in North India and Gujarat. It's an organ meat that, when prepared correctly, becomes incredibly tender, flavorful, and deeply satisfying. Kaleji Fry is liver that's been marinated in a blend of spices, then cooked to a perfect balance of crispy exterior and tender interior.
