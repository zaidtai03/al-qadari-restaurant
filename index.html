A Legacy of Flavour

Al Qadri is more than a restaurant — it's a family institution in the heart of Bilimora. For over a decade, we have been crafting recipes that bring people together, blending authentic Indian curries with bold Chinese flair.

Our kitchen is built on passion, fresh ingredients sourced daily, and the belief that every guest deserves a meal they'll remember. From our signature Amba Hardar Gravy to the beloved Desi Ghee Khichdi, every dish carries our soul.

Our Famous Signatures

House Favourite
Mamna
A cherished family recipe — tender, slow-marinated chicken prepared with an exclusive blend of spices that keep guests coming back. This is the dish Bilimora knows Al Qadri for.
Chef's Signature
Amba Hardar Gravy
A rich, golden gravy made with raw mango and turmeric that delivers a uniquely tangy and aromatic experience unlike anything else. Available with chicken, mutton or fish.
Comfort Classic
Desi Ghee Khichdi
Slow-cooked lentil and rice khichdi finished generously with pure desi ghee. Comforting, wholesome and soul-warming — a true Gujarati classic done with love.
