🥘 Amba Hardar Gravy

Chef's Signature • People's Favorite

What is Amba Hardar Gravy?

Amba Hardar is Al Qadri's crown jewel — a sophisticated, golden gravy made from raw mango and turmeric that delivers an experience unlike anything else you've tasted. The word "Amba" means mango in Gujarati, and "Hardar" refers to the bright turmeric hue. This unique combination creates a tangy, aromatic, and deeply satisfying curry.

Why It's Special

🥭 Raw Mango Magic
The secret ingredient is raw mango (amba), which gives the gravy its distinctive tangy sweetness and complex flavor profile that no other dish can replicate.

✨ Chef's Signature Creation
This is not a common curry found in every restaurant. Amba Hardar is Irsad Tai's personal creation, refined over years to achieve the perfect balance of tang, spice, and richness. It's a reflection of his culinary vision.

🌟 Versatile Excellence
While Amba Hardar started with chicken, its popularity led us to offer it with mutton and fish as well. Each protein brings out different notes in the gravy — the tenderness of chicken, the depth of mutton, and the delicate sweetness of fish. Try all three to experience the full spectrum.

🎯 Perfect Balance
The gravy is neither too spicy nor too mild. It's rich and creamy with a beautiful golden color, and the tanginess of raw mango cuts through the richness perfectly, making every bite refreshing despite the indulgence.

The Amba Hardar Experience

When you serve Amba Hardar, it arrives in a beautiful golden hue with tender meat swimming in the aromatic gravy. The aroma is intoxicating — hints of mango, turmeric, and warming spices. The first spoonful is an awakening: tangy, aromatic, and deeply flavorful. The tenderness of the meat combined with the silky gravy creates an unforgettable moment.

People's Favorite Choice

Amba Hardar has garnered a loyal following for good reason. It's the dish that makes people close their eyes and smile. It's the curry that gets ordered by repeat customers who know that if they're coming to Al Qadri, Amba Hardar is non-negotiable. It's the dish they recommend to friends and family with genuine enthusiasm.

Authentic & Premium Quality

Every ingredient in Amba Hardar is sourced fresh daily. The raw mangoes are hand-selected for ripeness and flavor. The spices are hand-ground to order. The meat is marinated for hours. Every step reflects our commitment to authenticity and excellence. This is why Amba Hardar tastes so remarkably different from other restaurants' attempts at similar dishes.

Amba Hardar isn't just a curry. It's a culinary journey, a moment of pure flavor indulgence, and the reason Amba Hardar Gravy has become synonymous with Al Qadri's name.
