What is Dal & Chaval?

Dal & Chaval is the essence of Indian home cooking — aromatic, spiced lentils paired with fluffy, fragrant rice. "Dal" refers to lentils (split chickpeas, kidney beans, or mixed lentils), cooked with traditional tadka (tempering of spices). "Chaval" is rice. Together, they form a complete, nutritious meal that's been the foundation of Indian cuisine for centuries.

Why It's Special

🌾 Complete Meal in One
Dal & Chaval is a complete protein — the combination of lentils and rice creates all essential amino acids. It's nutritious, filling, and satisfying without being heavy.

✨ Traditional Tadka
Our dal is prepared with traditional tadka — whole spices (cumin, mustard seeds, curry leaves, dried chilies) are tempered in hot oil and added to the cooked lentils. This technique infuses maximum flavor into every spoonful. The rice complements this perfectly, creating a harmonious flavor balance.

🔥 Authentic Gujarati Preparation
Our Dal & Chaval follows traditional Gujarati methods — the dal is slow-cooked until creamy, the tadka is done perfectly, and the rice is cooked separately to maintain texture. This is not a rushed, mixed-together dish — it's prepared with the respect it deserves.

🍗 Non-Veg Options Available
While dal & chaval is traditionally vegetarian, Al Qadri offers both veg and non-veg versions. The non-veg option includes chicken or your choice of protein, adding richness and nutrition to the already-nutritious base.
