What is Chicken Dum Biryani?

Chicken Dum Biryani is Al Qadri's interpretation of one of India's most beloved rice dishes. "Dum" means to cook in a sealed vessel, trapping all the flavors and aromas inside. "Biryani" is an art form — fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced chicken, then slow-cooked on hot coals until every grain is infused with the essence of the meat and spices.

Why It's Special

🔥 Coal-Cooked Tradition
Our Dum Biryani is prepared on coal — a traditional technique that few restaurants still practice. This gives the biryani its authentic smoky depth and aromatic complexity that you simply can't achieve with regular stove cooking.

🌾 Fragrant Basmati Rice
We use only premium basmati rice, each grain separated and aromatic. The rice is partially cooked before layering, ensuring it absorbs the chicken's spices and flavors perfectly during the dum cooking process.

🍗 100% Pure Chicken
Each kilogram of our biryani contains 1kg of pure chicken — no filler, no shortcuts. The chicken is marinated in yogurt and spices for hours before being layered with the rice, ensuring tenderness and flavor penetration.

✨ Special Masala Blend
Our signature biryani masala is a closely guarded recipe. The exact combination of whole spices (cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, bay leaves) is hand-selected and freshly ground. This is what gives our biryani its distinctive taste.

The Dum Biryani Experience

When the sealed biryani pot arrives at your table and is opened, the aroma that escapes is unforgettable — fragrant rice, tender chicken, warm spices. The rice grains are separate yet flavored. Every piece of chicken is tender and infused with spice. The bottom of the pot has "tah dig" (crispy rice cooked on coal) — a prized component that adds texture and depth.
